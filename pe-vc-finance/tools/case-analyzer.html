      04 Answer scaffold

      The five questions, every time
      1. Capital structure: what's the equity check, and how much debt is realistic?
      2. Returns: what's the MOIC and IRR under base, downside, upside?
      3. Value-creation lever: growth, multiple, leverage, M&A — which one drives the thesis?
      4. Exit: who buys this, at what size, at what multiple, and why would they pay?
      5. Deal-killer risk: the one thing that, if it happened, makes this a 0.8x return?
