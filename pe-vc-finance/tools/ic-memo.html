IC Memo Builder

An IC reads the same seven sections in every memo. The fill-in is on the left; a printable preview is on the right. Print to PDF when done. Nothing is sent anywhere.

00 Header

01 Thesis (the one paragraph IC remembers)

02 Sources & uses ($M)

03 Returns

04 Three key risks

05 Diligence findings (top 3)

06 Deal team & conditions

Live preview

Why these seven sections Investment committees read memos in the same order, every time: thesis first to decide whether to engage, sources/uses and returns to anchor the math, key risks to know what to ask, diligence findings to sanity-check, and team/conditions to know who's on the hook. Anything else is appendix. The discipline is to keep all seven on a single page if you can.
