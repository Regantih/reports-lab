      The four ties Tie 1 · Balance sheet balances: Assets = Liabilities + Equity. If this fails, stop reading.
      Tie 2 · ΔCash on the BS = CFO + CFI + CFF: the cash flow statement explains the cash change.
      Tie 3 · Retained earnings rolls: RE(P2) = RE(P1) + Net Income − Dividends.
      Tie 4 · CFO reconciles: CFO ≈ NI + D&A − ΔWorking capital. Allow some headroom for accruals you don't see, but big gaps deserve a question.
      Eleven flags worth a question
      1. A/R growing faster than revenue → revenue may be channel-stuffed or non-cash.
      2. Inventory growing faster than revenue → product not selling, possible writedown.
      3. A/P shrinking while revenue grows → squeezing suppliers or pulling forward payments.
      4. Capex < D&A for several years running → underinvestment in the asset base.
      5. CFO << Net income → earnings are not converting to cash.
      6. Effective tax rate moving up or down sharply → discrete items, deferred reversals.
      7. Other assets / other liabilities growing fast → the catch-all is doing more work than it should.
      8. Goodwill growing without disclosed M&A → unusual.
      9. Cash growing while net income falls → could be one-off receipts or financing.
      10. Debt rising while interest expense falls → mix shift or capitalised interest.
      11. Operating ratios moving in unison and abruptly → a process change or aggressive accounting.
