The Numbers Behind the Deal.

A working report on private-equity and venture-capital finance. The formulas you need at the close table, the traps that show up in every diligence, and eight browser-native solvers — built for the report, runnable in the page — that turn the math from a slide into a tool you can actually use.

§ The Lab

Eight tools, designed for the work.

These are not calculators. Each one is a Claude-designed workflow: the formula, the guardrails, the red flags a junior associate should look up, and a short capability drill so you don't ship the answer without earning it. Everything runs locally — no server, no API key, no telemetry.

Tool 01 — Buyout
LBO · MOIC · IRR Solver

Enter purchase price, debt, EBITDA, multiple expansion, and exit year. Returns equity check, exit equity, MOIC, IRR, and a value-creation bridge: EBITDA growth vs. multiple expansion vs. debt paydown.

PE · Buyout
Tool 02 — Cap Table
VC Dilution & Option-Pool Solver

Pre-money, investment, option pool top-up. Computes post-money, price per share, founder dilution with and without the option-pool shuffle, and the cost of putting the pool pre-money.

VC · Round Mechanics
Tool 03 — Exit
Liquidation Preference Waterfall

Stacked preferences with multiples, participation, caps, and conversion logic. Sweeps exit value to show where preferred-converts-to-common kicks in and what the founders actually receive.

VC · Exit
Tool 04 — Diligence
3-Statement Consistency Checker

Paste an income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow. Checks the four ties — A=L+E, ΔCash, retained earnings, and cash-flow reconciliation — and lights up specific red flags with explanations.

Diligence · Quality
Tool 05 — QoE
QoE & Add-back Inconsistency Checker

Stress-tests an adjusted EBITDA bridge against a list of acceptable add-backs. Flags the moves that QoE providers most often roll back: founder comp, COVID, one-time items that aren't.

Diligence · QoE
Tool 06 — IC
IC Memo Builder

A structured memo with the seven sections an IC actually reads: thesis, sources/uses, returns, key risks, diligence findings, deal team and conditions. Live preview, exports as printable HTML.

Process · IC
Tool 07 — Case
Case Study Analyzer

Drop in a deal description. The analyzer surfaces the five questions every PE/VC interview hides under the prose: capital structure, exit, returns hurdle, value-creation lever, and the deal-killer risk.

Drill · Case
Tool 08 — Thesis
Sector Thesis Canvas

A one-page canvas: market geography, the structural why-now, the unit economics that have to be true, the wedge, and the kill criteria. Saved to your browser; export as JSON or printable.

Strategy · Thesis

§ How it's built

A solver is not a calculator.

Each tool exposes its formula plainly, names its assumptions, and includes guardrails that point at the most common mistakes. The point is not to do the math for you — it's to make the math visible and then put the harder questions on top of it.

No data leaves your browser Everything in the lab runs client-side. No analytics, no server, no telemetry. Inputs you type are not stored unless the tool is explicitly a canvas (the sector thesis), in which case it uses localStorage on your device only.
