§ About the lab

Reports Lab is a single-author workshop. Every report is researched and written from primary sources, illustrated with original SVG diagrams, and laid out by hand. There is no template, no CMS, and no boilerplate prose.

The aim is the kind of long-form writing that used to live in trade journals and now mostly lives nowhere — patient, image-first explanations of complex systems, written for people who want depth without filler.

Source code, content, and diagrams are open on GitHub. Each report is a static site you can download and host yourself.
