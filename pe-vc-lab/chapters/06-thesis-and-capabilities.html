Thesis & capability drills

A canvas for AI / deep-tech / space theses, and a ladder of drills you can do with each tool.

What a thesis is

A sector thesis is a falsifiable prediction about where capital should go for the next three to five years, written down before the deals arrive. It's not a market map — those go stale on the day they're printed. It's an ordered list of bets the world has to validate.

The Sector Thesis Canvas uses six fields:

  1. Macro change. The thing that's actually different in the world.
  2. Buyer. Who pays. Concretely — function, budget line, internal sponsor.
  3. Business model. How the buyer is charged. Recurring? Per-use? Take-rate?
  4. Defensibility. What stops a fast follower or a hyperscaler from eating the category.
  5. Disqualifiers. What we will not invest in, even if it looks adjacent.
  6. The next 18 months. The two or three companies we expect to surface that fit the thesis.

Capability drills

Most analyst training is osmosis. The Capability Drills tool maps explicit exercises to each lab tool and tags them by level:

  • Beginner. Can you input a real public company's S-1 numbers into the LBO/VC model and reproduce the IPO MOIC for early holders within ±10%?
  • Intermediate. Can you read a 10-K's balance sheet and flag two of the eight tells from Ch. 04 in under twenty minutes?
  • Expert. Can you write a 1-page IC memo from a real founder pitch deck, identify the three failure modes, and predict which the founder will struggle to defend in the meeting?

Drills are deliberate, repeatable, and graded — the only way the lab gets better.
