The IC memo, in seven blocks

A structure that survives the worst meeting on the calendar. Use it; the IC Memo Builder exports the blocks straight to Markdown.

  1. The recommendation. One sentence at the top. Approve / pass / approve with conditions, with the cheque size and the price.
  2. The thesis. Three sentences. What the company is, what's changing in the world, why the company wins because of that change.
  3. The numbers. Today's revenue, today's growth, today's margin. The three-year pro-forma. The MOIC and IRR at base, downside, and upside.
  4. The diligence. What we did, what we found, what's still open. List open items, don't bury them.
  5. The risks. The three failure modes that would cost us money, ranked by probability × severity, with the mitigant for each.
  6. The structure. Cheque size, security, board seat, governance, follow-on reserves.
  7. The path. What happens in the first 90 days post-close. The first KPI we'll judge ourselves against.

The point of the structure isn't elegance — it's that every IC has read it before, so they don't waste time hunting for the recommendation, and they can challenge the parts that need challenging.
