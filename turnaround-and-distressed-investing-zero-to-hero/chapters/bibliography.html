Bibliography

Sources used in the report, organised by category. Where a public URL is stable, it is linked; where the source is print or paywalled, the citation alone is provided.

Core Practitioner Texts

  • Gatto, Michael. The Credit Investor's Handbook: Leveraged Loans, High Yield Bonds, and Distressed Debt (Wiley, 2023). The modern standard; written by a Silver Point partner who previously built credit training programmes at Citibank.
  • Moyer, Stephen G. Distressed Debt Analysis: Strategies for Speculative Investors (J. Ross, 2004). Still the reference technical text on valuation and claims analysis.
  • Whitman, Martin J., and Fernando Diz. Distress Investing: Principles and Technique (Wiley, 2009). The value-investing lineage applied to credit.
  • Rosenbaum, Joshua, and Joshua Pearl. Investment Banking: Valuation, LBOs, M&A, and IPOs (Wiley, 3rd ed., 2020). Chapter on restructuring and the LBO mechanics the distressed analyst needs.

Case Law and Market Developments

  • In re Serta Simmons Bedding, L.L.C., No. 23-20181 (5th Cir. Dec. 31, 2024). Uptier held not an 'open market purchase'; plan indemnity excised; equitable mootness rejected.
  • Ocean Trails CLO VII v. MLN TopCo Ltd. (Mitel), No. 2024-00169 (N.Y. App. Div. 1st Dep't Dec. 31, 2024). Uptier upheld on different contract language.
  • Harrington v. Purdue Pharma L.P., 603 U.S. ___ (2024). Non-consensual third-party releases held outside the Bankruptcy Code's authorisation.
  • Jones Day. Uptiers in 2025: Impact of the Serta and Mitel Decisions on Liability Management Exercises. Link ↗
